Frequently Asked Questions

What types of cases do you handle?

Worth Demands handles a range of personal injury matters involving documented medical treatment, including motor vehicle collisions, premises liability, and other negligence claims. Medically complex files are especially well suited to the service.

What materials do you need?

At minimum, medical records, billing documentation, and available incident or liability information. Additional supporting materials may also be incorporated when available.

What is your typical turnaround?

Most demands are completed within 3-5 business days, depending on file size and complexity. Larger or medically complex cases may require additional time.

How are revisions handled?

Requested factual corrections, additional records, and reasonable presentation changes are incorporated before finalization.
