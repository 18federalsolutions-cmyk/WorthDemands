FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of cases do you handle?

Worth Demands handles personal injury matters involving documented medical treatment, including motor vehicle collisions, premises liability, and other negligence claims. Medically complex and multi-provider files are especially well suited to the service.

What materials do you need?

Medical records, billing documentation, and available incident or liability information. Police or incident reports, photographs, imaging reports, wage-loss documentation, future-care recommendations, and other supporting materials may also be incorporated when available.

How are confidential case materials transferred?

Confidential case materials are exchanged separately through a secure document-transfer process rather than through the public website form.

What is your typical turnaround?

Most demands are completed within 3-5 business days, depending on file size and complexity. Larger or medically complex cases may require additional time.

How are completed demands delivered?

Completed demands are delivered in editable format and can be prepared using your firm’s preferred formatting and letterhead for final attorney review and submission.

How are revisions handled?

Requested factual corrections, additional records, and reasonable presentation changes are incorporated before finalization.

Can Worth Demands accommodate recurring or higher-volume work?

Yes. Worth Demands works with both individual assignments and recurring demand volume. Workflow and turnaround expectations can be coordinated based on your firm’s needs.
